Heritage & Legacy

Milestones in
Automotive
History

The rarest automobiles in history were not simply manufactured. They were agonised over, hand-built by artisans who devoted careers to the pursuit of mechanical perfection. Each milestone represents a moment when human ambition outpaced convention.

1936
Bugatti Type 57 SC Atlantic
Only 4 were built. One vanished during WWII. At auction, chassis 57374 sold for an estimated $40 million — the holy grail of automotive art.
1962
Ferrari 250 GTO
36 units. The most valuable production car ever. A 1963 GTO sold privately in 2018 for $70 million, setting the all-time record for any car at auction.
1992
McLaren F1
106 units. Held the production car speed record for 9 years at 386 km/h. A 1994 example sold for $20.5 million — proving timeless engineering never depreciates.
2005
Bugatti Veyron
450 units. The car that redefined what was possible. 1,001hp, top speed of 407 km/h, and a development cost of $1.5 billion — sold at a loss on every single car.
2021
Rolls-Royce Boat Tail
Three units. The most expensive new car ever produced at $28 million each, heralding a new era of ultra-bespoke coachbuilt automobiles for the ultra-wealthy.
